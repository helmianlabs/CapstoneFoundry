Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my desktop for Helmion Hands?
Launch Helmion.exe on your local workstation, enter your account pairing token from the web console, and authorize the local spoke. Hands commands will then relay directly between your phone and your desktop.

Where is my memory stored?
All conversation records, technical filings, and code decisions are encrypted in dedicated PostgreSQL databases with per-tenant isolation.
